The metrical approach

Six falling beats in English

Homer’s dactylic hexameter is quantitative; this translation uses stress as its audible principle. Dactylic feet predominate, with contracted feet and spondaic weight where the line calls for them.

The governing pattern is an accentual counterpart to Homeric hexameter—not a claim that English reproduces ancient Greek vowel length.
